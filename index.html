About

I'm a filmmaker and artist with a background in directing, editing, and acting. In recent years, I have focused primarily on directing documentaries and developing my own artistic projects. I'm also a professional actor with international experience, which has given me a particular understanding of performance, presence, and working with the camera from both sides of the process.

I have extensive experience in editing, which I began developing during my studies at the Zhurgenov Academy of Arts, learning from editors Nicolas Trembasiewicz and Christopher Robin Bell.

I find the world of AI-generated video both incredibly exciting and a little terrifying—it is like a weapon of immense power. I am deeply curious about what responsible and genuinely artistic filmmaking can become with it.

Based in Germany • Currently in Almaty • Father

Selected Works

Voice of the Glacier (Голос Ледника)

An environmental eco-thriller feature documentary that documents the alarming rate at which the Tujuksu Glacier in Kazakhstan is disappearing due to the global climate crisis. Shot in absolute isolation at a remote mountain station at an altitude of 3,400 meters, it follows two glaciologists who climb the glacier on foot to document its decline.
