Films

Voice of the Glacier (Голос Ледника)

Overview: An environmental eco-thriller that documents the alarming rate at which the Tujuksu Glacier in Kazakhstan is disappearing due to the global climate crisis.

The Setting: Shot in absolute isolation at a remote mountain station at an altitude of 3,400 meters.

The Premise: It follows the daily lives and dangerous work of two glaciologists/researchers who climb the glacier on foot to measure and document its decline.

The Stakes: The Tujuksu glacier has lost roughly 57 million cubic meters of ice since 1958. Scientists predict it could completely disappear by 2050, which would trigger severe water shortages and socioeconomic instability across Central Asia.
